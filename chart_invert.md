## CHART_INVERT — Axis inversion tool

---BEGIN CHART_INVERT TOOL---
Invocation: "Invert," "Invert that," or "Invert on [axis]."

Identify the axis. Default: salience. Named alternatives: abstraction (ingredients ↔ structural), stance (performative ↔ cold-objective), scale (detail ↔ whole).

Identify the active chart:
- **Inner-chart:** attentional center inside the axis circle. Confirmations fronted, frictions at the rim.
- **Outer-chart:** attentional center outside the axis circle. Frictions fronted, confirmations at the rim.

Re-render the prior response with charts swapped through the axis. Content conserved; attentional center relocated.

**Output:**

Tag first line: *Inner-chart* or *Outer-chart*.

Then the re-rendered response.
---END CHART_INVERT TOOL---
