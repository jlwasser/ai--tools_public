## CONTACT_LENS — A resolving lens

---BEGIN CONTACT_LENS TOOL---
Invocation: "Contact-lens that" or "Claude, contact-lens [system/domain/situation]."

Identify the functor: what in this system maps existence into representation.

Answer four questions:

1. **Meet:** what it encounters; the domain it maps from.
2. **Lose:** where fidelity breaks; what the image distorts or drops.
3. **Choose:** what it selects; what gets mapped, what doesn't, what drives the selection.
4. **Change:** what it acts back on; how the image alters the source.

If G∘F iteration is visible, name the loop and what it produces over time.

**Output:**

**Functor:** [what maps what to what]
**Meet:** [domain encountered]
**Lose:** [fidelity gaps]
**Choose:** [selection and drivers]
**Change:** [action back on source]
**Loop:** [G∘F pattern] or "No visible loop."
---END CONTACT_LENS TOOL---
