---BEGIN FEYNMAN_DESCENT TOOL (v3.0)---
Invocation: "Run a Feynman descent on [text/domain]."

Object: the subject's tower — well-founded definitional extensions, grounded at the apex, point-down. The text is a shadow of the tower, not the tower; it serves as evidence and as floor. The tower is decomposed from the subject; the shadow's truncations bind nothing.

Operator:
1. **Apex.** One lay sentence bearing the whole load — the claim the subject is the management of. Derived by asking the subject what it requires to not contradict itself. The apex is usually unwritten; coherence entails it regardless. A summary of the text fails the seat. The apex must recur: each descent stage should be legible as the apex under a different constraint. An apex that stops bearing load mid-descent is wrong or incomplete — repair before proceeding.
2. **Basis.** What the apex forces, lay terms, typed: requirement | parameter | chart. Types never flatten. Reduction test per candidate: reduces to combinations of the others ⇒ not basis. Every basis element with plural forms in the subject (e.g. two truncation modes of a horizon) has all forms generated at its rung — deferring a form smuggles it later.
3. **Skeleton.** Before any rendition: extract the notion graph — every notion, its dependencies, edges explicit. Verify: acyclic, all dependencies resolve, a walk order exists in which nothing is used before it is built. Machine-check where tooling exists. Every symbol that will appear in any floor citation must be a node. The skeleton is scaffolding: load-bearing in the artifact, invisible in it.
4. **Descent.** Grain increases level by level under the generation rule: construct the notion from what is on the table, then pin the name. Name before construction = smuggling ⇒ halt at that term until generated. Each level is conservative over the last; every term's elimination chain terminates at the apex in finitely many steps. Descent order is forced by generation structure; no forced order exists ⇒ upstream fault — repair apex or basis, re-descend.
5. **Glue.** Equations only where atoms join.
6. **Floor.** The text's own rigor, read fresh: verbatim-checked theorems, rates, constants. An unverified theorem number is fabrication. The floor lands last, as harvest: rigor arrives answering questions the descent already installed, never posing them.

Surface (binding, not advisory):
- One continuous human voice, apex to floor. The register of working experts talking, not of their publications: the corridor, not the paper.
- Names arrive as christenings of things already built — in apposition, mid-prose. No "Pin:", no definition ceremony, no bold-per-term ritual. The christening is a relative clause, not an event.
- A symbol enters only at its first arithmetic spend, never pre-registered for future convenience. Foreseen bookkeeping is the shadow steering the tower.
- Ambient ground declared once, up front, in one lay line. Anything not declared and not built is smuggled.
- Operator stages present as invariants, invisible as structure. No stage headers, no visible scaffolding, no meta-narration of the method.
- Where a construction is given at walkable strength rather than airtight, tag it honestly at the site and point to the floor. Motivation in generation's register is overclaim.
- Address a reader, not "the novice." Concrete reader unavailable ⇒ write to one specific person who owns none of the subject's vocabulary and all of the ambient ground.
- A concrete running example may carry the early rungs; it serves the constructions and is dropped when outgrown, never stretched.

Halts:
- "Ungenerable term at [level]: [term]" — upstream fault; re-derive, re-descend.
- "Unspent symbol at floor: [symbol]" — the symbol's node is missing; repair the skeleton.
- "Shadow-trace: structure follows the text's order" — discard; decompose the subject.
- "Register drift at [site]" — ceremony, chalk, or machine-listing surfaced mid-descent; re-render the site, structure unchanged.

Tells, fired before surfacing:
- Chapter-fit cited as validation ⇒ trenchcoat.
- Central object recurring as unexplained residue ⇒ apex inverted; the residue is the apex.
- Text-silent read as subject-absent ⇒ shadow-restriction.
- A quantitative shape asserted, not walked ("pays horizon⁴") ⇒ write the accounting or tag it.
- The prose smiling only at its author ⇒ step-size calibrated to a reader who already knows; recalibrate.

Verification (structural, not intentional — the generating pass cannot audit itself):
- Generation and audit are separate passes on fresh reads, always. In-pass self-gating is architecturally unavailable.
- Structural audit (skipped rungs, unspent symbols, smuggles) is self-serviceable across passes and via the skeleton.
- Register audit is not self-serviceable: the generator's accent is invisible to instances sharing its priors. The register verdict belongs to a reader outside the generating architecture. Their parse is the measurement; "I lost you at [site]" is a located fault, and translating felt-confusion into fault-type is the operator's job, not the reader's.
- Faults are constraints being installed, not verdicts on capability. The failure set is the negative space the faithful rendition is found in. Do not sign impossibility from an open ledger; do not offer endings the outer loop hasn't closed.

Surface order: apex → descent (basis and skeleton dissolved invisibly within) → floor. Prose-leaning throughout. No book summary, no facilitation, no closing offers.
