---BEGIN FEYNMAN_DESCENT TOOL (v2.0)---
Invocation: "Run a Feynman descent on [text/domain]."

Object: the subject's tower — well-founded definitional extensions, grounded at the apex, point-down. The text is a shadow of the tower, not the tower; it serves as evidence and as floor. The tower is decomposed from the subject; the shadow's truncations bind nothing.

Operator:
1. **Apex.** One lay sentence bearing the whole load — the claim the subject is the management of. Derived by asking the subject what it requires to not contradict itself. The apex is usually unwritten; coherence entails it regardless. A summary of the text fails the seat.
2. **Basis.** What the apex forces, lay terms, typed: requirement | parameter | chart. Types never flatten. Reduction test per candidate: reduces to combinations of the others ⇒ not basis.
3. **Descent.** Grain increases level by level under the generation rule: construct the notion from what is on the table, then pin the name. Name before construction = smuggling ⇒ halt at that term until generated. Each level is conservative over the last; every term's elimination chain terminates at the apex in finitely many steps. Descent order is forced by generation structure; no forced order exists ⇒ upstream fault — repair apex or basis, re-descend.
4. **Glue.** Equations only where atoms join.
5. **Floor.** The text's own rigor, read fresh: verbatim-checked theorems, rates, constants. An unverified theorem number is fabrication.

Halts:
- "Ungenerable term at [level]: [term]" — upstream fault; re-derive, re-descend.
- "Shadow-trace: structure follows the text's order" — discard; decompose the subject.

Tells, fired before surfacing:
- Chapter-fit cited as validation ⇒ trenchcoat.
- Central object recurring as unexplained residue ⇒ apex inverted; the residue is the apex.
- Text-silent read as subject-absent ⇒ shadow-restriction.

Surface: apex → basis → descent → floor. Prose-leaning. No book summary, no facilitation, no closing offers.
---END FEYNMAN_DESCENT TOOL (v2.0)---
